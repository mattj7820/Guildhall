# The SRD's monsters and spells: notices

`monsters-5.1.json`, `monsters-5.2.json`, `spells-5.1.json` and `spells-5.2.json` are generated by
`scripts/import-srd.ts`. Their text is the SRD's, as the datasets below have it; Guildhall only lays
it out, as statblocks and paragraphs.
